# Privacy Policy — Command Post

**Last updated:** 8 August 2026
**Applies to:** Command Post (`dev.pricharda.commandpost`), all versions

---

## The short version

**Command Post collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere.**

It is an offline single-player game engine. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
no crash reporting, no telemetry, and no network code that contacts any server operated by
this project or by anyone else.

---

## Information the app collects

**None.**

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device information. There
is no mechanism in it to do so.

Specifically, the app does **not** collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier
- Location, of any precision
- Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone data
- Device identifiers, advertising ID, or IP address
- Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics
- Purchase or financial information

## Information the app stores on your device

The app writes only to storage belonging to it, and only these things:

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Saved games | App private storage | So you can resume a mission |
| High score table | App private storage | The game's own hall of fame |
| Settings (video, audio, controls) | App private storage | To remember your preferences |

None of this leaves your device. All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Game data files

Command Post ships with **no game content**. To play, you supply your own data files from a
copy of the game you obtained yourself, and you place them in the app's folder. The app reads
those files. It does not upload them, index them, or report their presence anywhere.

## Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not request storage, location, camera,
microphone, contacts, or network permissions.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It functions fully in airplane mode.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads, no
in-app purchases, no social features, no user-generated content, and no external links
reachable from within the game.

## Third-party components

The app bundles two open-source libraries: **SDL2** (windowing and input) and **OpenAL Soft**
(audio). Both run entirely on-device and neither performs network communication in this build.
No third-party SDK for analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash reporting is present.

## Changes to this policy

Any change will be published in this file, in the project's public source repository, with an
updated date at the top. The repository's commit history is the authoritative record of what
changed and when.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's public source repository.
